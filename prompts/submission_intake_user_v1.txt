SCREENING POLICY V1

SC01 — Company and site identification
Pass: the proposer and proposed insured site can be identified,
subject to the explicitly permitted synthetic-case exception.
Unknown: either cannot be identified reliably.
Fail: the source explicitly establishes that there is no
identifiable proposer or insured site for this submission.

SC02 — Territory
Pass: the insured site is stated to be in the UK.
Fail: it is stated to be outside the UK.
Unknown: territory cannot be established.

SC03 — Business activity
Pass: the stated business is furniture storage/distribution,
with no manufacturing or processing activity.
Fail: stated activities include manufacturing/processing or
a different business outside this demonstration's scope.
Unknown: the description is insufficient or materially conflicting.
Do not infer “no manufacturing” merely from its omission.

SC04 — Site and occupancy
Pass: the submission concerns one occupied site.
Fail: it explicitly concerns multiple sites or a wholly or
partly unoccupied site, outside this demonstration's scope.
Unknown: site count or occupancy cannot be established.
Do not confuse a warehouse extension with a second site.

SC05 — Requested cover
Pass: property damage insurance is requested.
Fail: the submission explicitly requests only other cover.
Unknown: the requested cover cannot be established.

SC06 — Essential information quality
Pass: supplied text is sufficiently readable and SC01–SC05
can be evaluated without unresolved essential contradictions.
Fail: the source explicitly states that essential submission
information is unreliable or unusable.
Unknown: missing, unreadable or conflicting essential information
prevents reliable screening.

Non-blocking flags:
Record relevant outstanding information, such as protection
coverage, panel specification, storage height, repair evidence,
valuation basis or business interruption calculation.
These do not automatically fail the basic screen.
